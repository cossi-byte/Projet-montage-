# Balou l'éléphant — Bible de production (LOT 1)

Les prompts d'image des épisodes utilisent des **balises** : remplacez chaque balise par le bloc anglais ci-dessous avant de coller dans le générateur. Ainsi les personnages restent identiques d'un plan à l'autre.

| Balise | Bloc à coller (anglais) |
|---|---|
| [STYLE] | soft rounded 3D cartoon render for toddlers, bright pastel colors, warm golden light, gentle soft shadows, cute and friendly, 16:9 |
| [BALOU] | Balou, a small chubby round baby elephant, light blue-grey skin, big round ears pink inside, big shiny round brown eyes, short trunk with a little curl at the tip, pink cheeks, small tuft of hair on his head, sunshine-yellow overalls with a star-shaped button, red sneakers |
| [MAMAN] | Mama Elephant, a gentle adult elephant, light blue-grey skin, kind smile, green headscarf with white flowers |
| [ZAZOU] | Zazou, a wise friendly giraffe, round glasses, blue scarf |
| [PICO] | Pico, a tiny perfectly round yellow bird, small orange beak, tiny wings, big happy eyes |
| [HIPPO] | Mister Hippo, a round purple hippopotamus wearing a red-and-white striped apron |
| [VALLEE] | the Baobab Valley, a colorful little village by a calm blue lake, round baobab-tree houses with round doors and windows |
| [MARCHE] | the little fruit market of the Baobab Valley, colorful wooden stalls with red-and-white striped awnings, baskets of fruit |
| [LAC] | the sandy shore of a calm sparkling blue lake with lily pads and a small wooden jetty |
| [MAISON] | Balou's house, a big round baobab tree with a round blue door, round windows and a small flower garden with a white fence |

**Prompt négatif (à ajouter partout)** : no text, no letters, no scary elements, no sharp teeth, no extra limbs, no realistic style.

**Générique (10 s, identique pour chaque épisode)** — IMAGE : `[STYLE] [BALOU] slides down a pastel rainbow into [VALLEE] and lands waving with his trunk, wide shot` · VOIX : aucune · SON : jingle joyeux ukulélé + trompette jouet (titre « Balou l'éléphant » ajouté au montage).

**Voix** : Balou = enfant joyeux, légèrement aigu, 10 mots max par phrase. Maman = douce et calme. Zazou = posée, sage. Pico = rapide, pétillant. Monsieur Hippo = grave, jovial.
